# Example Prompts

Replace the example referral codes with the identifiers for your task.

## Before Replying to a Trader

> Use `sai-deals` to brief me on `EXAMPLE-ATLAS`. The trader asked whether their volume milestone unlocked a withdrawal. Find the term, show the latest measurement and its cutoff, then check for an executed payment or unlock. Tell me what the recorded form of payment allows. Give me two sentences I can use in my reply, plus the record IDs and dates behind them. Flag anything we still need to confirm.

## Daily Comms Review

> Use `sai-deals` to find completed payments or setup actions from the past seven UTC days that still need trader follow-up. Check deal-level notes before putting a record in the queue. For each remaining item, show the stable deal ID, event date, evidence, communication status, and one proposed next step. Show undated records separately and list any deals you could not check.

## Ambiguous Wallet

> Use `sai-deals` to find the deal for the wallet I provided. If it belongs to several deals, list their stable deal IDs and matching aliases and ask me which deal to review. Keep payments tied to the deal they came from.
